REGIONAL ROUTE ENQUIRIES

Transport within and between regions

Ask about movements between the locations below or share another destination. A city name alone is not enough to confirm pickup or delivery access.

South India

For Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, share your exact collection and delivery points. Examples include Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), Kochi, Chennai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

For industrial loads, include factory access and loading facilities. For car or bike movements, include the model and handover requirements.

West and Central India

For Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh enquiries, specify both endpoints, such as Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Indore or Raipur.

Tell us whether the cargo is loose, packed, machinery or a container, and whether a particular truck body or trailer is required.

North India

For Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and surrounding northern destinations, share the full address and PIN code. Enquiries may include Delhi, Jaipur, Ludhiana or Lucknow routes.

For hill locations in Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir or Ladakh, describe road width, gradients and any known vehicle-access restrictions so feasibility can be reviewed.

East and Northeast India

For West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand, Assam and other northeastern destinations, provide the district and exact pickup or delivery location. Examples include Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, Patna, Ranchi and Guwahati.

For remote or hill destinations, discuss the receiving point, local access and whether any additional handling or onward arrangements are needed before booking.

Villages, smaller towns and other destinations

Your village or district does not need to appear in this list to make an enquiry. Enter its name, district, state and PIN code in the transport enquiry form, with a nearby landmark where useful.

Explain the last stretch of the route: approach-road width, loading space, turning space and access for the proposed vehicle. Our team will confirm whether address pickup or delivery can be arranged, or discuss an alternative handover point.

Island and access-restricted locations

Road transportation alone cannot complete every journey. For island destinations or locations requiring another mode of transport, ask the team to assess feasibility and clarify any separate arrangements. Do not assume a standard road transport quote covers those stages.
