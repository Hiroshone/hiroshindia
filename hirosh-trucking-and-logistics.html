Hirosh Trucking and Logistics Pvt Ltd serves the logistics requirements of businesses operating in domestic and international markets. Our business areas span transportation, freight forwarding, cargo handling, warehousing, supply chain management and distribution.

Our Business Areas

Transportation & Multimodal Logistics

Coordination of goods movement by road, rail, air and sea, including connections between transport modes for domestic and international shipments.

Freight Forwarding & Cargo Handling

Shipment coordination and cargo-handling arrangements based on the origin, destination, cargo characteristics and delivery requirements.

Warehousing & Cold Chain

Storage, cold-storage and associated cargo-movement solutions, with facilities, capacity and temperature requirements confirmed for each assignment.

Supply Chain & Inventory Management

Support for coordinating inbound supplies, inventory movement, storage and outbound deliveries across business locations.

Distribution & Delivery

Distribution planning, courier and delivery coordination for commercial requirements, including movements between factories, warehouses, distributors and customers.

Industrial & Project Logistics

Transport and handling coordination for machinery, equipment, oversized cargo, factory relocation and project-site deliveries.

For Corporate Clients

We welcome enquiries from manufacturers, exporters, importers, distributors, retailers and organisations seeking logistics support across multiple locations.

Requirements may include recurring transport movements, factory dispatches, warehouse transfers, distribution operations and project-specific assignments. Service scope, reporting requirements, handling responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed according to the engagement.
