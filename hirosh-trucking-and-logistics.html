THE COMPANY BEHIND HIROSH ROADWAYS

Hirosh Trucking and Logistics Pvt Ltd

Hirosh Roadways is operated by Hirosh Trucking and Logistics Pvt Ltd. We coordinate transport and relocation requirements across India through a nationwide network of agents and transport partners.

One point of coordination for your movement

Customers can approach Hirosh Roadways with a commercial consignment, machinery requirement, vehicle move or relocation project. Our team gathers the pickup and delivery details, assesses the cargo and coordinates suitable transport arrangements through the network.

A useful transport plan identifies the equipment, loading method, receiving-site access and handling responsibilities. We ask for these details before confirming the scope so that a truck journey, a machinery move and a complete relocation are quoted according to their actual requirements.

Transport, logistics and relocation

Our existing services include truck and trailer arrangements, commercial goods transport, Full Truck Load and Part Truck Load, express cargo, container and reefer transport, machinery movements and ODC cargo enquiries. Industrial packing, machine dismantling, factory and plant shifting, household and furniture moves, office relocation, car and bike transportation and vehicle-storage enquiries are also part of the service range.

Vehicle types, specialist equipment, storage locations and handling services are confirmed for the individual requirement. A nationwide service network does not mean company-owned offices or facilities in every location.

Coverage built around the customer's location

Our main office is in Kazhakuttom, Thiruvananthapuram, with an office near Vizhinjam port. The network supports enquiries from across India, including industrial estates, business towns and district locations. Use the state guides to find district coverage and selected industrial pickup points, then provide your actual site address.

Island destinations, remote roads and oversized cargo need specific access and onward-transport planning. Any additional transport leg or handling arrangement is confirmed separately before booking.

A clear scope before dispatch

Tell us the cargo description, package count, dimensions, weight, pickup readiness and final delivery address. For machinery, include lifting and dismantling requirements; for relocation, provide an inventory and access details. The quotation should identify the agreed transport and handling scope, payment terms and applicable charges.
