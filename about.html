ABOUT HIROSH ROADWAYS

Kerala roots.
Nationwide road transport.

Hirosh Roadways is a Goods Transport Agency (GTA), coordinating pan-India transportation since 2017 through a strong agent and transport-partner network.

A team for the whole journey.

Based in India, with our main office in Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), Kerala State. We coordinate road transportation across India, with services connecting India, Nepal and Bangladesh.

Truck and trailer rental and ODC cargo transportation are core services. We handle oversized machinery, equipment and industrial loads with movement requirements assessed for each shipment. Customers also come to us for car and bike transportation, commercial goods, container and reefer transport, industrial packing and moving, machinery relocation, household and office shifting.

Our teams coordinate enquiries, vehicle arrangements, pickup, movement updates and delivery requirements through our transport-partner network. Our main office is in Kazhakuttom, with an office near Vizhinjam port.
