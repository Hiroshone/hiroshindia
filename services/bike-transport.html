Bike transport enquiries in Trivandrum

Arrange motorcycle or scooter transportation enquiries with Hirosh Roadways in Kazhakuttom, Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum). Share your pickup location and destination in India so our team can check the available transport and handling arrangements through our partner network.

What information is needed for a quote?

Share the bike make and model, whether it is in running condition, pickup and delivery PIN codes, preferred date and packing requirements. Tell us about access restrictions or assistance needed at either location. For an electric two-wheeler, mention that it is electric when enquiring so the team can review carrier requirements.

Are pickup, packing and delivery included?

These arrangements depend on the route and quotation. Ask which pickup point will be used, what packing is proposed, and whether delivery is to an address or an agreed collection point. Confirm the scope and charges before booking.

What should I prepare before handing over the bike?

Ask the team to confirm the documents and carrier preparation requirements for your movement. Record the bike’s condition and agree how keys and any accessories will be handed over. Keep the booking and handover details for reference.
