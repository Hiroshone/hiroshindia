Truck and lorry booking in Trivandrum

Contact our Kazhakuttom team for truck rental and lorry booking enquiries in Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), including movements to other locations across India. We coordinate vehicles through our transport-partner network; the vehicle, pickup point and schedule are confirmed for each shipment.

What should I share for a truck quote?

Tell us the pickup and delivery PIN codes, cargo type, package count, total weight, largest item dimensions and preferred date. Mention narrow approach roads, loading space and whether loading equipment or labour is needed. If you are unsure which truck to choose, our team can assess the details with you.

Where is the booking office?

Our main office is at TC 1/418, Kinfra International Apparel Park Road, Kazhakuttom, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala 695582. Contact the team before bringing cargo so the collection point and handling arrangements can be confirmed.

What affects the transport charge?

The route, vehicle type, load dimensions and weight, pickup access and handling requirements affect the quote. Ask the team to clarify the included services, any additional charges and the expected collection and delivery schedule before booking.
