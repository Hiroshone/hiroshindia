ODC Cargo Transportation

Specialised transport for oversized cargo.

Hirosh Roadways handles Over-Dimensional Cargo (ODC) transportation across India. We coordinate oversized machinery, equipment and industrial cargo movements around the dimensions, weight, route and site requirements of each load.

How we can help

  • Over-dimensional cargo transportation
  • Oversized machinery and equipment
  • Trailer selection and movement planning
  • Route and site-access assessment

ODC transportation in India: plan around the load

ODC transport requires the cargo dimensions, total weight and route to be reviewed together. Share the transport-ready length, width and height, including packing or supports. Photographs or drawings can help our team assess trailer coordination and the handling required at both sites.

Before confirming an oversized cargo movement

Discuss the proposed vehicle, route assessment, site access, loading and unloading facilities, and any permissions or escort arrangements that the movement may require. The team will clarify the agreed scope and responsibilities in the quotation.

For project cargo moving in several loads, include the equipment list and delivery sequence. Related requirements can be discussed through our industrial machinery transport and trailer transportation services.

Our team will confirm the scope, availability, price and schedule for your requirement before booking.
