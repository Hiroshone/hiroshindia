Our Business Areas

Transportation & Multimodal Logistics

Freight Forwarding & Cargo Handling

Shipment coordination and cargo-handling arrangements based on the origin, destination, cargo characteristics and delivery requirements.

Warehousing & Cold Chain

Storage, cold-storage and associated cargo-movement solutions, with facilities, capacity and temperature requirements confirmed for each assignment.

Supply Chain & Inventory Management

Support for coordinating inbound supplies, inventory movement, storage and outbound deliveries across business locations.

Industrial & Project Logistics

Transport and handling coordination for machinery, equipment, oversized cargo, factory relocation and project-site deliveries.
