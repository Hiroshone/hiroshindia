Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Pithampur consignments, state the sector, unit and gate so the pickup is not treated as an Indore city collection.
  2. For Mandideep machinery, provide a machine list, packed sizes and lifting arrangements at both ends. State whether packing or dismantling is part of the request.
  3. For mixed commercial goods, list package dimensions and stackability alongside weight. This helps assess FTL versus PTL without assuming that a light load occupies little truck space.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
