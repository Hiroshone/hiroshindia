Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. State the island, receiving jetty or premises and the cargo’s packed size. Confirm who is arranging the sea leg and port handling before requesting mainland collection.
  2. For machinery or vehicles, check acceptance, handling and documentation with the relevant carrier before dispatch. A road-transport quotation should not be assumed to include sea freight.
  3. Agree the final handover point, storage or waiting arrangements and delivery responsibility in writing. Availability of each service below must be checked for the island and shipment.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
