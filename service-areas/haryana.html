Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Manesar factory pickup, provide sector, plot and gate details together with the loading slot. For a car move, identify whether the vehicle is running and whether carrier access is possible.
  2. For Bawal or Kundli equipment, share packed dimensions and weight per piece. Lifting equipment and loading supervision must be agreed at both ends.
  3. For Haryana-to-Delhi deliveries, confirm the final receiving address and applicable vehicle-entry conditions before dispatch. A fixed delivery promise should follow route and vehicle assessment.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
