Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Jaipur industrial shipments, identify Sitapura, VKIA or the relevant estate instead of a general city address. Share loading hours and the vehicle space inside the premises.
  2. For Bhiwadi or Neemrana machinery, include packed dimensions, weight and lifting points. Check that unloading equipment is ready before agreeing a dispatch date.
  3. For long-distance district deliveries, give the last accessible truck point and any onward handling requirement. Packing and weather protection should match the cargo rather than the distance alone.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
