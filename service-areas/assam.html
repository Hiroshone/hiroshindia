Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Amingaon or Chaygaon collection, name the estate and unit rather than using Guwahati alone. Share loading hours and cargo readiness.
  2. For food products, specify packaging, shelf-life handling and any temperature requirement. A reefer arrangement is confirmed only after the cargo specification is reviewed.
  3. For onward deliveries to other northeastern states, identify the final destination, not just an Assam transfer point. Handling and onward vehicle suitability should be agreed before dispatch.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
