Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Navi Mumbai or Panvel container enquiries, include the actual terminal or CFS, container size and empty-return point. This avoids treating an industrial-estate delivery as a port collection.
  2. For Pune-region factory dispatches, confirm the MIDC estate, gate booking and crane or forklift provision. Trailer dimensions and turning space matter for long machinery.
  3. For Kolhapur, Nashik or other district pickups, compare FTL and PTL using the actual weight, volume and delivery requirement. A shared-load option should not be assumed suitable for fragile or oversized cargo.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
