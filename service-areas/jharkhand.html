Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Adityapur machinery, list each piece and whether it can travel on its own base or needs a crate. Agree lifting and lashing responsibility before dispatch.
  2. For Bokaro or other plant deliveries, obtain the gate instructions and receiving contact. A city address is not a substitute for plant-entry details.
  3. For factory relocation, separate equipment transport from dismantling, packing and positioning. The quotation should identify which tasks are included.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
