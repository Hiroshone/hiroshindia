For office or household moves, include the floor, lift and vehicle parking point. For Vizhinjam-linked cargo, identify the port or CFS gate and container return requirement.

Kollam

Kollam, Karunagappally, Kottarakkara, Punalur, Paravur, Chavara, Kundara.

Separate the town pickup address from the factory or warehouse entrance. Share package count and loading arrangements for goods transport, or an inventory for household shifting.

Pathanamthitta

Pathanamthitta, Thiruvalla, Adoor, Pandalam, Ranni, Konni, Mallappally.

For deliveries beyond the main towns, send a map pin and the last approach-road details. Confirm whether the truck can reach the premises or needs a smaller onward vehicle.

Alappuzha

Alappuzha, Cherthala, Aroor, Kayamkulam, Chengannur, Mavelikkara, Haripad.

Identify the road-accessible loading point for coastal and inland deliveries. For furniture or packed commercial goods, specify protection and unloading requirements.

Kottayam

Kottayam, Changanassery, Pala, Ettumanoor, Vaikom, Erattupetta, Kanjirappally.

For multi-stop collections, give each address and package inventory separately. Machinery and commercial loads need dimensions as well as total weight.

Idukki

Thodupuzha, Kattappana, Munnar, Adimali, Nedumkandam, Kumily.

Hill-route enquiries need an exact location and suitable vehicle-size assessment. Share access photos for narrow approaches and confirm a receiving contact before dispatch.

Ernakulam

Kochi, Kalamassery, Aluva, Angamaly, Perumbavoor, Muvattupuzha, Kothamangalam, Tripunithura, Eloor, Edayar.

Industrial packing, machine movements and business deliveries are assessed using the actual unit address. Port-linked movements also need terminal, release and empty-return details.

Thrissur

Thrissur, Chalakudy, Irinjalakuda, Kodungallur, Kunnamkulam, Guruvayur, Wadakkanchery.

For shop, office and household moves, include the access window and inventory. Discuss whether loading can happen at the building or at an agreed nearby parking point.

Palakkad

Palakkad, Kanjikode, Ottapalam, Shoranur, Pattambi, Mannarkkad, Chittur.

Factory pickups around Kanjikode need the unit gate and lifting plan. For other district locations, provide the full address so the collection vehicle and onward route can be assessed.

Malappuram

Malappuram, Manjeri, Perinthalmanna, Tirur, Ponnani, Nilambur, Kottakkal, Kondotty, Tanur, Tirurangadi.

Provide individual cartons or equipment dimensions when requesting FTL or PTL. Car and bike transport enquiries should identify the accessible handover location and vehicle condition.

Kozhikode

Kozhikode, Feroke, Ramanattukara, Koyilandy, Vadakara, Mukkam, Kinalur, Kuttiyadi.

For commercial collections, identify the warehouse or shop and loading time. Distinguish a town-centre pickup from an industrial-unit gate when requesting the vehicle.

Wayanad

Kalpetta, Sulthan Bathery, Mananthavady, Meenangadi, Vythiri.

Hill approaches and final-site access should be checked before selecting a large vehicle. Include cargo dimensions, receiving arrangements and any unloading equipment needed.

Kannur

Kannur, Thalassery, Payyanur, Taliparamba, Mattannur, Iritty, Kuthuparamba.

Business deliveries, vehicle moves and relocation enquiries can use these local pickup areas. Give the actual premises and loading inventory rather than only the nearest town.

Kasaragod

Kasaragod, Kanhangad, Nileshwaram, Uppala, Manjeshwaram.

For interstate movements towards Karnataka or other destinations, identify both ends of the journey. Confirm vehicle access and the receiving contact for delivery beyond the main town.

These are service-enquiry locations supported through our agent and transport-partner network. Vehicle availability and handling arrangements are confirmed for your address and consignment.

Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Vizhinjam or Kochi-linked container movements, specify the pickup terminal or CFS, container size, cargo weight, delivery address and empty-return location. Port handling and road transport must be identified separately in the quotation.
  2. For Aroor or Pallippuram food consignments, confirm whether temperature control is required. Share the temperature range and loading readiness before requesting a reefer; ordinary covered transport is not a substitute for a cold chain.
  3. For hill destinations and narrow residential roads, send the final address, access photographs and unloading space. A smaller final-delivery vehicle may need a separate arrangement.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
