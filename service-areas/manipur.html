Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Takyelpat collection, give the estate unit, cargo dimensions and loading contact. Agree packing and lifting scope for machinery.
  2. Before setting dispatch dates, confirm current route access and the receiving site’s readiness with our team. Do not rely on a generic statewide transit estimate.
  3. For hill-district deliveries, share the final road approach and unloading method. If a smaller onward vehicle is needed, its scope should be agreed separately.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
