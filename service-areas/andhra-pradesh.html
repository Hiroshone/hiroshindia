Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Sri City, identify the exact unit and whether the movement involves an SEZ gate. The shipper should confirm the dispatch documentation and permitted collection time.
  2. For port-linked loads near Visakhapatnam, Kakinada or Nellore, specify whether the request is for a loaded container, loose cargo or an empty container. Include the release point and empty-return instructions.
  3. For equipment moving between Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu, Telangana or Karnataka, provide a complete loading and unloading scope. Dismantling and positioning work should be agreed separately from the road journey.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
