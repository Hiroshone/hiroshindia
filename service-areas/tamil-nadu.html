Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Gangaikondan pickups, give the SIPCOT unit address and identify whether the machine is crated, dismantled or transported as one piece. This determines the loading method and vehicle selection.
  2. For a factory move involving Hosur, Sriperumbudur or Coimbatore, separate the machine inventory from office furniture and loose stores. Confirm who handles disconnection, lifting, packing and reinstallation before pricing.
  3. For Chennai or Thoothukkudi-linked container transport, supply the terminal/CFS and empty-return instructions. A town name alone does not identify a container collection point.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
