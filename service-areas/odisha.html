Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For a Kalinganagar equipment movement, state the receiving plant, cargo size and handling method. Large project loads require route and site assessment.
  2. For Paradip-linked container or cargo movement, distinguish terminal collection from a factory pickup and provide release and empty-return details where applicable.
  3. For Mancheswar commercial dispatches, list packages, weight and loading readiness. Delivery timing depends on the agreed vehicle and receiving-site arrangements.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
