Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For cargo collected from Naroda, Vatwa or Sanand, state the actual estate and unit number. Ahmedabad alone is not a sufficiently precise truck collection address.
  2. For drums, liquids or chemical-related cargo, disclose the contents and handling documentation before vehicle allocation. Acceptance depends on cargo suitability and the agreed transport arrangement.
  3. For machinery or packed industrial products, give dimensions, package count and unloading equipment. Container-related enquiries need the terminal and return location as well as the inland delivery point.
  4. For Mundra, Kandla, Hazira or Dahej-linked movements, identify the terminal/CFS, loaded or empty status, container size and gross weight. Agree the road-transport scope, release readiness, return point and any waiting charges; port handling or sea freight is a separate requirement.
  5. For Morbi or Thangadh ceramic products, provide carton or pallet counts, packed dimensions, total weight and stacking instructions. Confirm the receiving equipment and unloading responsibility so fragile cargo is not treated as ordinary loose goods.
  6. For Rajkot, Vadodara or Jamnagar machinery, list each machine separately with its packed dimensions and weight. Share photographs, lifting points and whether dismantling, packing or positioning is needed.
  7. For collections from multiple Gujarat suppliers, list each estate, unit and loading slot. Ask for a plan that accounts for the order of pickups, load compatibility and available truck space rather than assuming one city-wide collection.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
