Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For warehouse pickup at Batasingaram or Mangalapally, confirm the dock slot, pallet count and whether the load can be stacked. Waiting time and unloading responsibilities should be agreed before dispatch.
  2. For pharmaceutical or other sensitive cargo, state the required temperature, packaging and handling instructions. Reefer availability and suitability are confirmed for the shipment rather than assumed from the location.
  3. For Nizamabad or Nalgonda enquiries, share the exact pickup village or town and delivery address. The district list supports enquiries beyond Hyderabad without implying a local Hirosh office in every district.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
