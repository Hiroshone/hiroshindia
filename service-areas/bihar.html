Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Bihta pickups, distinguish the industrial area from the mega industrial park and provide the actual unit address.
  2. For Hajipur or Barauni goods, state package count, weight, dimensions and whether the receiving site has unloading equipment.
  3. For district deliveries beyond the main town, include the village, landmark and PIN code. Confirm the last truck-accessible point before scheduling a large vehicle.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
