Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Naharlagun or Pasighat collection, identify the unit and cargo readiness. Include packed dimensions, weight and lifting requirements.
  2. For remote or hill deliveries, give the actual site, route landmarks and unloading space. A district headquarters address cannot represent every village approach.
  3. Confirm applicable access permissions and current route conditions before dispatch. The quotation should state the agreed final delivery point and any onward handling.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
