Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Byrnihat or Umiam factory loads, supply the unit, loading method and receiving-site equipment details.
  2. For hill-town deliveries, confirm the final access road and available turning space. Large equipment may need additional route assessment.
  3. For retail or office goods, identify package count, weight and carrying distance from vehicle to premises. Separate packing and manual handling from the road transport scope.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
