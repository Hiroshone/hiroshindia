Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Rangpo or Singtam industrial cargo, state the unit, loading access and packaging requirements. Sensitive products require a specific handling brief.
  2. For Gangtok or other hill destinations, share the final road and unloading space. A vehicle suitable for an approach town may not suit the last leg.
  3. Ask for a shipment-specific schedule after route and weather conditions are checked. For machinery, provide every piece dimension and weight before a trailer decision.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
