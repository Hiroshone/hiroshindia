Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Bengaluru office relocation, identify floor access, lifts, dismantling work and receiving-site hours. Industrial machinery requires a separate dimensions and lifting assessment.
  2. A Peenya-to-interstate enquiry should include the factory gate, packed load dimensions and forklift or crane availability. Do not assume that the same loading arrangement exists at the delivery end.
  3. For Mangaluru and other coastal deliveries, confirm weather protection and the exact unloading point. For estates outside Bengaluru, use the estate name and PIN code to avoid a city-centre routing assumption.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
