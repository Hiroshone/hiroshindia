Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Ludhiana machinery, include the focal-point phase, machine dimensions and whether packing or dismantling is required.
  2. For goods collected from several suppliers, list each pickup separately. Multiple stops and loading time should be agreed before selecting FTL or PTL.
  3. For Mohali office relocation, share the floor, lift availability, furniture inventory and receiving-site timings. Packing and access needs differ from industrial cargo.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
