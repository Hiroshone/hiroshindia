Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Bari Brahmana or Lassipora machinery, state the loading gate, dimensions and handling equipment. Industrial entry and unloading should be confirmed by the receiving unit.
  2. For movements between Jammu and the Kashmir region, ask for a route-specific schedule after current road conditions are checked.
  3. For cars and household goods, share the exact handover point and delivery access. Packing, unloading and any onward transfer should be clear in the agreed scope.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
