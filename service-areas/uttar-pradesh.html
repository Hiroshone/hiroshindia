Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Surajpur or other NCR pickups, confirm the estate/site number, entry timing and exact gate. Access requirements should be checked for the chosen vehicle.
  2. For packed products, include cubic dimensions as well as weight. Fragile items and non-stackable packages can change the vehicle requirement substantially.
  3. For machinery delivered outside the city, share an access map and unloading plan. Trailer and ODC enquiries are assessed using the full route and load dimensions.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
