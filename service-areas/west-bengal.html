Plan the pickup, journey and delivery

  1. For Panagarh or Kharagpur pickups, share the park, unit and loading gate. Factory dispatch slots and handling equipment should be confirmed before vehicle arrival.
  2. For Kolkata or Howrah urban deliveries, identify access limitations and the unloading point. A long trailer may need a different plan from a small commercial vehicle.
  3. For an onward movement from Siliguri into hill destinations, provide the final location and cargo size. The onward route and vehicle suitability need a separate check.

Send the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, package dimensions, weight and preferred date. Our team confirms vehicle availability, handling responsibilities, access and quotation terms before booking.
